Project preparation

Make your first conversation more useful.

You do not need all the answers. A little context helps identify the right questions.

  1. Your website URL and the services or products that matter most.
  2. The audience and locations you want to reach.
  3. Your main challenge and what a useful outcome would look like.
  4. Recent website changes, migrations or known technical issues.
  5. Existing analytics or search reports, if available.

Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

Do I need every SEO service?

No. The right scope depends on your website and goals. A focused audit or one service may be an appropriate starting point.

Can we implement recommendations ourselves?

Implementation responsibilities can be agreed as part of the project. Explain your team's capabilities and availability during the initial conversation.

What should I expect from an SEO report?

A useful report connects completed work, relevant performance signals and next steps. It should also explain uncertainty and tracking limitations.

Can you guarantee a ranking or deadline?

Search results depend on many factors beyond a single website. Scope, deliverables and review points can be agreed, but a fixed ranking or results timeline should not be assumed.
